Process

How a project actually moves.

Four stages, roughly 10 to 20 weeks depending on scope.

01

Discovery

A site visit and a long conversation about how you live — not a style questionnaire.

02

Concept

Floor plans, material palette, and 3D renders presented as one coherent proposal.

03

Execution

Procurement, contractor coordination, and weekly site supervision through to handover.

04

Styling & Handover

Final furnishing, art, and a walkthrough — plus a maintenance guide for every material used.
